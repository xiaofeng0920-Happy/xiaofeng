## What the pilot covers

For a small business that repeatedly combines exported order files:

- Up to 3 Excel/CSV files, up to 10,000 rows in total and 20 columns per source table.
- Agree the field mapping, unique row key, cancellation/refund treatment and output before work starts.
- Merge and normalize the data, preserve identifiers and original files, and flag duplicates or conflicting values for review.
- Deliver cleaned detail, product-level quantity/amount summaries, an exception list and a reusable processing workflow with brief instructions.
- Include one revision within the agreed scope and a repeat run on a second batch of the same format.
